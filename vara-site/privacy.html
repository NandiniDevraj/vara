Privacy.

Last updated October 15, 2026

This page explains what this website collects, who handles it and what you can ask me to do with it. The short version: I only collect what you choose to send me, and I use it to reply to you.

Who I am

Vara is run by Nandini Devaraj. You can reach me at devarajnandini23@gmail.com with any question about this page.

What I collect

The contact form asks for your name, your email or phone number, and optionally your business name, website address, what you need help with, your budget range and a message.

The review form asks for your name, your business, a star rating, your review and your permission to publish it.

I do not have user accounts, and this site has no advertising or analytics tools at the time of writing. It does not set cookies of its own.

How I use it

Contact details and project details are used to reply to you and to plan and deliver the work you ask for. A review is published only if you tick the permission box, exactly as you wrote it, with the name and business you gave. I do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who handles it

Form messages are sent through Formspree, a form service that passes them to my email and may keep a copy in its own systems under its own privacy policy. If that service is unavailable, the form opens your email app instead, and your email provider then handles the message.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses when pages load.

Content loaded from other companies

Every page loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google and shares your IP address and browser details with it. The home page also loads an animation library from cdnjs, run by Cloudflare, which receives the same details. I do not control what these companies do with that information, so please see their own privacy policies.

Browser storage

The site uses your browser's session storage to remember that a page transition is playing and that you closed the offer pop-up. It holds no personal information and is cleared when you close the tab.

How long I keep it

I keep inquiries for as long as I need them to reply and to keep business records, and I delete them when you ask. A published review stays on the site until you ask me to take it down.

Your choices

You can email me to ask what I hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to remove a review you gave. I will reply within two working days.

Changes to this page

If I add tools such as analytics or change how the site works, I will update this page and the date at the top.
